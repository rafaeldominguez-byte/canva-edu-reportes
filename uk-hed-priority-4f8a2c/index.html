Method and caveats

The four signals

  1. Unmanaged adoption — active users in the last 12 months, on the institution's domains and located in the United Kingdom, who are not on Canva Enterprise, Canva Campus, Canva for Education or Canva for Districts. This is commercial whitespace: it measures demand that has never been offered an institutional agreement.
  2. Coverage gap — for existing and at-risk customers only: active users minus the seats actually provisioned, where provisioned is the largest of contracted seats, seats in use and active users already sitting on an institutional bundle. Taking the largest of the three means the gap can only ever be understated.
  3. Potential plan alignment — seats on plans awarded against eligibility criteria (Canva for Education gratis, not-for-profit gratis) that sit on a higher-education domain. Counted only on brands holding at least five users from the institution's own domains: a lone verified teacher on a Canva for Education plan is normal and is deliberately out of scope.
  4. Low priority — fewer than 300 active users outside an institutional plan and no material eligibility-based free seats.

The score

score = active users outside coverage × usage intensity × commercial readiness × confidence weight

  • Usage intensity = (designs created in 12 months ÷ active users) ÷ the median across the 64 institutions (10.73 designs per active user), clamped between 0.5 and 2.0.
  • Commercial readiness = 1.50 live contract with a named Salesforce owner · 1.25 prior or won agreement not currently live · 1.15 significant out-of-pocket paid usage already inside the institution · 1.00 no commercial relationship yet.
  • Confidence weight = 1.00 high · 0.85 medium · 0.60 low.

How the dominant play is chosen

  1. C — Eligibility review if the institution is a university, is not a customer, and carries either 50+ Canva for Education gratis seats or 100+ not-for-profit gratis seats.
  2. A — Expand if it is an existing or at-risk customer with 300+ active users outside coverage.
  3. B — New logo / re-engage if there are 300+ active users outside coverage and no live agreement.
  4. D — Low priority otherwise.

An institution can carry several signals at once. Play C institutions also appear in the commercial ranking, and every institution with eligibility-based free seats appears in the watchlist regardless of its dominant play.

Confidence criteria, as recorded in the data

  • Unmanaged adoption — high: 500+ active users measured from live feature-bundle membership · medium: 100–499 · low: fewer than 100.
  • Coverage gap — high: live subscription with a seat count plus a validated Salesforce account · medium: validated Salesforce account and a won opportunity but no live subscription linked to the domain brands · low: inferred from domain evidence only.
  • Potential alignment — high: Canva for Education gratis with 100+ seats on a university domain, at least 20 of the institution's own people inside the brand, and no further-education or partner-school explanation visible · medium: material eligibility-based seats on a higher-education domain supported by a single line of evidence · low: small volume, a non-university institution, or a legitimate explanation already visible in the data. Confidence is automatically reduced when the institution is a further-education college, when the domain set contains sub-domains that look like partner schools or academies, when a meaningful share of the users self-declare as teachers, or when the Salesforce match was already flagged as ambiguous.

What this report does not say

  • Free usage is organic demand, not misalignment. The 112,815 active users with no institutional coverage are the opportunity, not a problem to be corrected.
  • Eligibility signals are hypotheses pending validation of the FE and HE rules. The current Canva for Education and not-for-profit eligibility criteria for UK further and higher education have not been applied to these rows; the C4E/NFP team owns that judgement.
  • A single domain can host education faculties, partner schools and colleges, charitable foundations, students' unions, alumni bodies and staff with a dual role. Seats on an eligibility-based plan under a university domain are therefore expected to a degree, and the numbers here are an upper bound on anything that might need resizing.
  • Teacher and student labels are self-declared at sign-up. They are directional, never proof.
  • Two Salesforce matches remain open and were deliberately kept out of the customer status: University of York International Pathway College (a Kaplan joint venture) and University of Greater Manchester (whose Salesforce website field points at manchester.ac.uk). Both need a human call.
