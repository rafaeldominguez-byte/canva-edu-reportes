Method and scope

How Canva for Campus works

The institution buys staff licences on Canva Enterprise; the student full-time-equivalent (FTE) count sets the minimum staff licences. Once that minimum is met, the whole student body unlocks free on a separate student team — all or nothing, and the students cost nothing. Canva Education is a separate product for schools and is not part of this model.

Student FTEMinimum staff licences
0 – 2,49950
2,500 – 4,999100
5,000 – 10,000150
10,001 – 15,000250
15,001 – 20,000500
20,001 – 25,000750

Definitions

  • Students entitled: the student FTE the agreement unlocks. Students provisioned: students actually inside the student team today. Activation = provisioned ÷ entitled.
  • Staff licence utilisation: staff licences in use ÷ staff licences bought.
  • Domain claim: a user counts toward an institution when their account sits on one of the institution's verified email domains; it says nothing about who administers that account.
  • Student accounts vs the student body: the numerator is student-role accounts accumulated across cohorts (graduates keep their accounts); the denominator is this year's student FTE (HESA). Because a stock is being compared with a one-year snapshot — and FTE undercounts part-time headcount — the ratio can exceed 1. Up to 100% it is shown as a percentage; above it, as an amber multiple ("1.2× the student body") so nobody reads it as coverage. It signals accumulated adoption, and it is never used in scoring.
  • Status: customer state from the organisation layer and Salesforce (existing customer with its plan, won pending implementation, prospect, churned, no CRM record). "At risk" is deliberately not a status — renewal risk is what Play B captures.

How the suggested action is generated

  • Transparent rules over the data itself, not free text: Play A customers renewing in ≤45 days with <5% student activation → provisioning conversation before renewal; under 50% activation → activation plan with IT. Play B with seats bought but nothing deployed → escalate implementation; renewal in ≤60 days → prepare with the utilisation story; ≥95% utilisation → expansion conversation; <60% → adoption push. Play C prospects with more active staff than required licences → formal proposal; 70–100% of the minimum → staff-activation push; below → nurture. Play D → route to the C4E/NFP eligibility review.

Scoring and selection

  • Score = 100 × play weight × intensity × confidence weight. Play weight: A 1.00 · B 0.90 · C 0.70 · D 0.50. Confidence weight: high 1.00 · medium 0.85 · low 0.60. The default view shows the Top 20 by score; use the controls to change depth, sorting and filters — the previous fixed quota-based Top 20 is superseded by this configurable view.
  • ARR (known) is the amount of the last won Salesforce opportunity per account — never an estimate — and is pending confirmation with Bush and Petia before this leaves the team. Anomalous amounts (e.g. blended staff+student deals) are noted per institution in the accordion.
