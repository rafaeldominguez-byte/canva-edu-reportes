Canva across UK higher education: customers and opportunities

⚠️ Internal use only — do not share this URL; each institution receives only the direct link to its own report

Metrics count users whose email sits on each institution's official domains — root domains plus every sub-domain carrying at least 25 users — and who are located in the United Kingdom (last refresh shown on each card). Where the worldwide figure for the same domains is higher, it is shown next to the domain chips as “N worldwide”. Status comes from three signals combined: a live Canva subscription linked to a Salesforce account, a live institutional bundle (Canva Enterprise, Canva Campus or Canva for Districts) with at least five domain members, and the Salesforce account type. “Seats used / contracted” compares the seats occupied by people on official domains with the seats on live subscriptions. “On institutional plans” counts users on Canva Enterprise, Canva Campus, Canva for Districts or Canva for Education — it is the governance signal for this system; there is no single-sign-on metric, because that field is not populated in the warehouse. Out-of-pocket spend is what individuals paid themselves, in US dollars (US$) — it is not an institutional licence. MAU = monthly active users; the current month is always partial, and July and August sit outside term time, so monthly active figures run low. Internal document of the Canva Higher Ed EMEA team; aggregated figures only, no personal data.
